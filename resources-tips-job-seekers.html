Tips for job seekers

Write a resume park owners notice, and prepare for the interview.

Make your resume park-ready

  • Lead with a short summary: the role you want, where you're willing to work and when you're available.
  • List hands-on skills — reservations systems, trades, cleaning standards, first aid, forklift or chemical handling.
  • Mention if you're a couple applying together, and what each of you brings.
  • Keep it to two pages and use plain formatting.

Before the interview

  • Look up the park: its size, accommodation types and guest reviews.
  • Prepare examples of handling a difficult guest or a busy period.
  • Ask about accommodation, rosters, peak seasons and pets if relevant.

After you apply

Follow up politely after a week if you haven't heard back. Keep your resume listing current so parks see you're still available.
